## Built on Neurodesk

Sciget extends [Neurodesk](https://neurodesk.org), the open-source platform for biomedical imaging analysis published in Nature Methods.

- 200+ containerised, version-pinned scientific tools.
- 3,800 monthly users across 100+ countries.
- Australian iAwards 2025 winner — Best Technology Platform.
- Backed by $2.5M+ AUD from ARDC, CZI, Wellcome Trust, and NIF.

[Visit Neurodesk →](https://neurodesk.org)
