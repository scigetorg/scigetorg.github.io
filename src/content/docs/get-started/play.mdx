Sciget Play gives you the Sciget analysis environment in your web browser. With Play you can:

- Start using Sciget without installing anything.
- Use a wide range of preinstalled scientific tools.
- Try the platform before you install it locally.
- Work with colleagues in the same environment.

:::note
Sciget Play is free but has resource limits. For heavier workloads, [install the Sciget App](/get-started/scigetapp/) or [choose another setup](/get-started/).
:::

## Launch Sciget Play

The tool below checks how quickly each Play server responds from your location. Select the **Recommended** card to start.

:::

## Alternative: run a web app on your own computer

For single tasks, Sciget Webapps run directly in your browser on your own computer. Play gives you a complete desktop on a hosted server. Webapps let you open one tool without starting a Play session or installing anything, and your data never leaves your machine. Browser and GPU requirements vary by app.
