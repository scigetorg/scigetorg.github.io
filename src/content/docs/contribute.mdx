Sciget is a community-engaged catalogue. Anyone can propose, package, and contribute a scientific tool. Submissions are peer-reviewed before publication.

## Submission workflow

<Steps>

1. **Package your tool.** Build a container recipe with our easy-to-use recipe maker.
2. **Add documentation.** Provide clear instructions and example datasets.
3. **Review.** Your submission undergoes peer review before publication.

</Steps>
