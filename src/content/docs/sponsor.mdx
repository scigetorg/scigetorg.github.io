SCIGET is an open-source platform developed by the team behind [Neurodesk](https://neurodesk.org) at The University of Queensland. We rely on grants, sponsorships, and community donations to cover our cloud hosting, container build, and distribution infrastructure costs.

Financial donations for SCIGET are currently processed through The University of Queensland's **Neurodesk** fund, which directly supports the shared team and open infrastructure powering both Neurodesk and SCIGET. Choose the option below that matches your location:

## For supporters outside the United States

Donate securely through UQ Giving: <a href="https://donations.uq.edu.au/EAINNEUR" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><strong>Donate via UQ Giving</strong></a> (opens in a new tab).

## Institutional and infrastructure sponsorship

Organisations, research facilities, and cloud or CI providers can also sponsor SCIGET through compute credits, build infrastructure, hosting, or institutional partnerships. Start a conversation on [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/orgs/scigetorg/discussions) to connect with the maintainers.

See the grants, research clouds, and sponsors already supporting this work on our [Acknowledgements](/acknowledgements/) page.

## Thank you

Thank you for supporting open scientific infrastructure. If you are using a Neurodesk / Neurodesktop session, you can run this command inside your environment to hide the donation reminder banner:

```bash
touch ~/.config/neurodesk_supporter
```
